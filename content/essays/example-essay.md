This is an example essay. This is an example paragraph. Blah blah blah.

The **title**, **subtitle**, **name**, **date** and **tags** are all visible here. The **section** and **subcategory** you pick decide where it's listed, if you feel strongly about where your piece belongs feel free to include that but if not we can also place it where we feel is appropriate. The subcategory doesn't appear on the page itself it's only for the site to put it in the correct subcategory and visible when someone clicks that subcategory in the bar under essays. If you upload an **image**, it appears between the subtitle and the start of the essay, like the placeholder above.

Lines can have breaks, like this.

You can put words in *italics* with single asterisks and in **bold** with double asterisks. You can also link to things: just like: [this link to the submission guidelines](/about).

> Pull quotes are also possible!

A quote can also be more than one paragraph long:

> This is the first paragraph of a longer quote.
>
> Pressing enter inside the quote starts a second paragraph.

## Section headings

Start a line with two number signs and a space to make a heading like the one above.

- Start a line with a dash and a space to make a bullet point.
- Each new line that starts with a dash is another point in the same list.

1. Numbered lists work the same way, start each line with a number and a period.
2. The numbers count up on their own.

* * *

Three asterisks with spaces between them, alone on a line, make a section break like the one above. 

For writers, don't worry too much about formatting when submitting an essay (this is more for editors to do) but just know that these are all features you can include in your essay if you feel like it adds to it! Email us if you think there's something speciifc you may want and we can work on getting it implemented.
